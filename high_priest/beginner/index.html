High Priest: Beginner Guide to Hell 15

From your very first Acolyte run to Hell 15: Priest with Magnus Exorcismus first, then Rebirth into High Priest and the Heal x Lex Aeterna build. No MVP cards, Star Potential up to purple (Epic). Midgard Survivors 0.2.8.

1. How a run works

  1. You only move. Your character attacks by itself.
  2. Monsters give EXP. Every level-up you pick one upgrade: a new skill, a skill level or a stat.
  3. A run lasts up to 40:00. The map's MVP (a big boss) shows up during the run. Beating the MVP clears the map.
  4. Chests drop gear. Their rank goes up every 3 minutes of the run, so later chests are better.
  5. When you beat the MVP you may take 3 items to the Kafra Storage. That is how you keep gear forever.
  6. You start as Acolyte. At level 20 of a run you change job. High Priest is not open yet: you get there with a Rebirth (section 5).

5. The road to High Priest

1Acolyte: every run starts here. 2Priest at run level 20 (pick Priest, not Monk). 3Rebirth at the Valkyrie in Prontera. 4High Priest: from now on, pick High Priest at level 20.

What Rebirth needs

  • Acolyte class level 30. Class level grows with every Acolyte run you play (it is not the level inside a run).
  • 4 Ymir Fragments. You get one every time you beat a map's MVP.

What Rebirth gives

  • High Priest (and Champion) at level 20, and +2 to all stats from the start.
  • The soul slot and +1 legendary in your starting gear (2 in total).
  • +1 passive slot. The class level starts again at 1 (new cap 50).

6. Two phases: Magnus first, Heal later

The real build is Heal x Lex Aeterna: Heal procs explode, and Lex Aeterna multiplies the explosions. That needs the Soul of Quadrille, and Altair only sells it after the Rachel Sanctuary quest (Danger 12). Until then you clear with the Priest's own skills.

Phase 1: Magnus (Danger 1 → 12)

Magnus Exorcismus (free at the job change) puts a holy cross on the ground that keeps hitting everything on it. With Aspersio, monsters you kill with your basic attack burst into holy light that jumps to the next ones. Lex Aeterna marks the strongest monsters: the next skill hit on them deals double or more.

Phase 2: Heal x Lex (Danger 12 → Hell 15)

Buy the Soul of Quadrille from Altair. Every Heal is now a holy explosion for three times what it heals, your cards cast Heal all the time, and every 4 basic hits Aspersio puts a Lex Aeterna mark that the next explosion multiplies (×17 or more at level 150).

Getting the 10 Legendary Coins

From about 3:20 into a run, chests can drop legendary gear (rare, gold name). Keep the ones you will use (Odin Mask, Odin's Blessing) and sell every other legendary at the Kafra: each one gives 1 Legendary Coin. Start saving from your first runs, so the coins are ready when Altair opens.

Lucky shortcut: the Quadrille mace itself can drop for Acolyte characters. If it does, equip it right away (it uses your legendary slot): Heal starts exploding even before Rebirth. Abstract can open 2 card slots on it for Mastering.

How the Heal x Lex build kills

14 Mastering + 8 Vitata cast Heal2Soul of Quadrille: each Heal explodes3Aspersio: every 4 hits, a Lex Aeterna mark4The next explosion on the mark is multiplied

So: no cards that cast other skills. Any other skill hit would spend the Lex Aeterna mark on a weak hit.

8. Gear, step by step

Where gear comes from

  • Every piece here is a normal chest drop. Morning Star, Belt, Manteau and Boots are rank 2: they show up after the first few minutes of a run.
  • Keep pieces by taking them to the Kafra Storage after an MVP win. Prefer pieces with 3 card slots; Abstract opens the 4th.
  • Cards in kept gear are Eternal Cards: fuse 5 copies (10 for boss cards) in your album.
  • Refine: +7 is enough for Hell 15.

Star Potential

Altair opens Star Potential at the same time as the soul. This guide only needs purple (Epic), and only one line:

  • +4% all stats on every piece, 3 lines each. By far the best purple line here: DEX, INT and LUK all feed the explosions.

Traits: Resonant (procs trigger more often) on every piece that can take one, as in Mochi's build.

10. Climbing to Hell 15

  • Survive to 40:00. More time means more levels, and levels are your damage.
  • The start of every run is the weak part. Before the job change at level 20 you have no Magnus and no explosions. Play safe and kite until then.
  • Keep hitting the MVP. Lex Aeterna marks come from your basic attack, and Vitata casts Heal when you are hit: stay close with Vengeance.
  • Every MVP is a Ymir Fragment until your Rebirth, and every legendary you won't use is a coin. Nothing you find is wasted.
  • Climb one step at a time: when you win easily, go up. If you die early, farm the previous level for cards and refines.

6. Duas fases: Magnus primeiro, Heal depois

A build de verdade é Heal x Lex Aeterna: os procs de Heal explodem e o Lex Aeterna multiplica as explosões. Isso precisa da Soul of Quadrille, e o Altair só vende depois da quest do Rachel Sanctuary (Perigo 12). Até lá você limpa com as skills do próprio Priest.

Fase 1: Magnus (Perigo 1 → 12)

O Magnus Exorcismus (grátis na troca de classe) coloca uma cruz sagrada no chão que fica acertando tudo em cima dela. Com o Aspersio, os monstros que você mata com o ataque básico explodem em luz sagrada que pula para os próximos. O Lex Aeterna marca os monstros mais fortes: o próximo golpe de skill neles causa o dobro ou mais.

Fase 2: Heal x Lex (Perigo 12 → Hell 15)

Compre a Soul of Quadrille do Altair. Agora todo Heal é uma explosão sagrada de três vezes o que ele cura, suas cartas lançam Heal o tempo todo, e a cada 4 golpes básicos o Aspersio coloca uma marca do Lex Aeterna que a próxima explosão multiplica (×17 ou mais no nível 150).

Como juntar as 10 Moedas lendárias

A partir de uns 3:20 de run, os baús podem soltar equipamento lendário (raro, nome dourado). Guarde os que vai usar (Odin Mask, Odin's Blessing) e venda todo o resto de lendário na Kafra: cada um dá 1 Moeda lendária. 10. Subindo até o Hell 15

  • Sobreviva até 40:00. Mais tempo é mais nível, e nível é o seu dano.
  • O começo de toda run é a parte fraca. Antes da troca de classe no nível 20 você não tem Magnus nem explosões. Jogue com calma e corra até lá.
  • Continue batendo no MVP. As marcas do Lex Aeterna vêm do ataque básico, e a Vitata lança Heal quando você apanha: fique perto com a Vengeance.
  • Todo MVP é um Fragmento de Ymir até o seu Rebirth, e toda lendária que não vai usar é uma moeda. Nada do que você acha é desperdiçado.
  • Suba um degrau por vez: quando vencer fácil, suba. Se morrer cedo, farme o nível anterior atrás de cartas e refino.

6. Dos fases: Magnus primero, Heal después

La build de verdad es Heal x Lex Aeterna: los procs de Heal explotan y Lex Aeterna multiplica las explosiones. Eso necesita la Soul of Quadrille, y Altair solo la vende tras la misión de Rachel Sanctuary (Peligro 12). Hasta entonces limpias con las habilidades del propio Priest.

Fase 1: Magnus (Peligro 1 → 12)

Magnus Exorcismus (gratis en el cambio de oficio) pone una cruz sagrada en el suelo que golpea sin parar todo lo que la pisa. Con Aspersio, los monstruos que matas con el ataque básico estallan en luz sagrada que salta a los siguientes. Lex Aeterna marca a los monstruos más fuertes: el siguiente golpe de habilidad sobre ellos hace el doble o más.

Fase 2: Heal x Lex (Peligro 12 → Hell 15)

Compra la Soul of Quadrille a Altair. Ahora cada Heal es una explosión sagrada de tres veces lo que cura, tus cartas lanzan Heal todo el tiempo, y cada 4 golpes básicos Aspersio pone una marca de Lex Aeterna que la siguiente explosión multiplica (×17 o más al nivel 150).

Cómo juntar las 10 Monedas legendarias

Desde unos 3:20 de partida, los cofres pueden soltar equipo legendario (raro, nombre dorado). Guarda los que vas a usar (Odin Mask, Odin's Blessing) y vende todos los demás legendarios en la Kafra: cada uno da 1 Moneda legendaria. Empieza a juntar desde tus primeras partidas, para tener las monedas listas cuando se abra Altair.

Atajo con suerte: la propia maza Quadrille puede caer para personajes Acolyte. Si cae, equípala enseguida (usa tu ranura de legendaria): el Heal ya explota antes del Rebirth. Abstract le abre 2 ranuras de carta para Mastering.

Plagiarism: Meltdown

Meltdown hace que el monstruo reciba más daño de todo. Cópiala una vez con una partida de Thief (o Rogue / Assassin) en Juperos (Schwarzwald, Peligro 9) derrotando a un Archdam, y ponla como habilidad compartida (Álbum → Plagiarism → Every class). Si ya lo hiciste para Champion, ya está compartida.

10. Subiendo hasta Hell 15

  • Sobrevive hasta 40:00. Más tiempo es más nivel, y el nivel es tu daño.
  • El inicio de cada partida es la parte débil. Antes del cambio de oficio en el nivel 20 no tienes Magnus ni explosiones. Juega con cuidado y corre hasta entonces.
  • Sigue golpeando al MVP. Las marcas de Lex Aeterna vienen de tu ataque básico, y Vitata lanza Heal al recibir golpes: quédate cerca con Vengeance.
  • Cada MVP es un Fragmento de Ymir hasta tu Rebirth, y cada legendaria que no uses es una moneda. Nada de lo que encuentras se desperdicia.
  • Sube un escalón a la vez: cuando ganes fácil, sube. Si mueres pronto, farmea el nivel anterior por cartas y refinado.

5. 轉職 High Priest 之路

1Acolyte：每場都從這裡開始。 2遊戲中 20 級轉 Priest（選祭司，不要選武僧）。 3在普隆德拉找 Valkyrie 轉生。 4High Priest：之後每場 20 級選 High Priest。

轉生條件

  • Acolyte 職業等級 30。職業等級會隨每場 Acolyte 遊戲提升（不是遊戲內的等級）。
  • 4 個尤米爾碎片。每打倒一次地圖 MVP 就得到一個。

轉生獎勵

  • 20 級可轉 High Priest（或 Champion），且一開始全能力 +2。
  • 靈魂欄位，以及起始裝備多 1 件傳說（共 2 件）。
  • +1 被動欄位。職業等級重新從 1 開始（新上限 50）。

6. 兩個階段：先 Magnus，後治療

真正的流派是治療 x Lex Aeterna：治療觸發會爆炸，Lex Aeterna 再放大爆炸。這需要 Quadrille 之魂，而 Altair 要完成 Rachel Sanctuary 任務（危險度 12）才會賣。在那之前，用 Priest 自己的技能清怪。

第 1 階段：Magnus（危險度 1 → 12）

Magnus Exorcismus（轉職時免費獲得）在地面放下神聖十字，持續攻擊上面的怪物。有 Aspersio 時，普攻擊殺的怪物會爆出神聖之光並跳到下一隻。Lex Aeterna 標記最強的怪物：下一次技能命中造成兩倍以上傷害。

第 2 階段：治療 x Lex（危險度 12 → Hell 15）

向 Altair 買 Quadrille 之魂。之後每次治療都是治療量三倍的神聖爆炸，卡片一直施放治療，而且每 4 次普攻 Aspersio 會留下 Lex Aeterna 標記，讓下一次爆炸放大（150 級 ×17 以上）。

收集 10 枚傳說硬幣

遊戲進行約 3:20 後，寶箱可能掉落傳說裝備（稀有，金色名稱）。保留會用的（Odin Mask、Odin's Blessing），其他傳說全部在卡普拉賣掉：每件換 1 枚傳說硬幣。從第一場就開始存，Altair 開放時硬幣就夠了。

幸運捷徑：Acolyte 角色也可能直接掉落 Quadrille 鈍器。掉了就馬上裝上（佔用傳說欄位）：轉生前治療就會爆炸。Abstract 可以幫它開 2 個卡槽放 Mastering。

Meltdown 讓怪物承受所有傷害提高。用 Thief（或 Rogue / Assassin）在 Juperos（Schwarzwald，危險度 9）擊敗 Archdam 複製一次，再設為共享技能（卡冊 → 剽竊 → Every class）。如果已經為 Champion 做過，就已經共享了。

10. 爬到 Hell 15

  • 撐到 40:00。時間越長等級越高，等級就是你的傷害。
  • 每場的開局最弱。20 級轉職前沒有 Magnus 也沒有爆炸，小心走位拉怪。
  • 持續攻擊 MVP。Lex Aeterna 標記來自普攻，Vitata 受擊會施放治療：有復仇契約就貼近打。
  • 轉生前每隻 MVP 都是一個尤米爾碎片，用不到的傳說都是硬幣。撿到的東西都不浪費。
  • 一階一階往上爬：輕鬆贏就往上；太早死就回前一級刷卡片與精煉。
