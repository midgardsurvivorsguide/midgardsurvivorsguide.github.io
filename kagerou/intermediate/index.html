Kagerou Bleed: Intermediate

The step after the beginner guide: clear Hell 15 with two Venom Knives, bleed and poison, using gear you can farm. No full S grade or perfect Star Potential needed.

This build

The goal: Hell 15 first

This guide is about finishing Hell 15 runs, not the Oath yet. Get comfortable here first: every clear gives better drops and more levels for your gear. When Hell 15 feels easy, start raising the Oath a few steps at a time.
1Bleed + poisonVenom Knives poison every hit; bleeding bursts do almost 80% of the damage.
2Shadows8 Kathryne Keyron + 4 Angeling: more hits per swing and more Mirror Image shadows.
3SurviveAngel's Kiss, Grace, Immortal Stew and First Aid keep the run alive to the end.

Proof: a Hell 15 clear

A tester took this build to Hell 15 Hard in Payon Cave: level 396, 33:41, rank 13 of 506 that month. Bleeding 78% of the damage, Mirror Image 10%. In their words: "It was a smooth run, although my FPS dropped. My gear isn't all S rank and the potentials are random, because I'm broke."

Gear

Everything +10 where it can be. Grade S and perfect Star Potential make it stronger, but the tester reached Hell 15 without them: aim for +7% all stats lines when you reroll.

How many Right Hand Mastery levels?

Bleed chance stops at 100%: past that every hit already bleeds, so extra chance is wasted. It adds up from Blood Pact (30%), Beelzebub Card (30% each) and Right Hand Mastery (10% at level 1, +5% per level). The more Beelzebub cards you use, the fewer RHM levels you need.

Beelzebub cardsWith Blood PactRHM must addRHM level for 100%
0×30%70%27
1×60%40%9
2×90%10%1
3×120%0%1 (damage only)

Each RHM level also gives +8% basic-attack damage, so a level or two over the line is not wasted.

Go full DEX

DEX with no limit, then LUK. AGI only to 10. The tester ended with DEX 273 and LUK 211.

Never

STR, VIT and INT. Ninja Aura already gives you plenty of STR, and the tester banished VIT too. Banish all three.

Kagerou 流血：中階

新手攻略的下一步：用雙 Venom Knife、流血與中毒打完 Hell 15，裝備都能刷到。不需要全 S 級或完美星之潛能。

這個流派

目標：先打完 Hell 15

這份攻略的目標是穩定打完 Hell 15，先不碰 Oath。先在這裡打熟：每次通關都有更好的掉落與更多等級。等 Hell 15 變輕鬆，再慢慢提高 Oath。

Right Hand Mastery 要點幾級？

流血機率最高 100%：超過後每一下都已經流血，多的就浪費了。來源是 鮮血契約（30%）、Beelzebub 卡（每張 30%） 與 Right Hand Mastery（1 級 10%，每級 +5%）。Beelzebub 卡越多，RHM 需要的等級越少。
