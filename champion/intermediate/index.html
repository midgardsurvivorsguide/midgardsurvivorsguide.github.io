Quadrille Champion: Intermediate (Hell 15)

The Heal-proc Champion with gear you can finish quickly: everything +7, no Star Potential and no wings. It clears Hell 15 and is the base you upgrade into the advanced build.

This build

The goal: clear Hell 15

This is not an Emperium Festival build. It is the cheap version that gets you through Hell 15 with weak gear, so you can farm refines, grades and MVP cards. For the Festival, use the advanced guide once your gear is complete.
1Heal procs4 Mastering cast Heal on your basic attacks; 8 Vitata cast it when you are hit.
2Heal explodesThe Soul of Quadrille turns each Heal into a holy explosion: almost all of your damage.
3Buffs multiplyOdin 4/4, Blessing, Steel Body, Signum Crucis EX and Meltdown make every explosion hit much harder.

Proof: a Hell 15 clear

Hell 15 in Glast Heim with Frenzy ×2, patch 0.2.8: Dark Lord, Fallen Randgris and Satan Morroc down, run finished at 20:20 on level 367.

Quadrille Champion：中階（Hell 15）

治療觸發 Champion，裝備很快就能湊齊：全部 +7、不需要星之潛能也不需要翅膀。能打完 Hell 15，也是升級到進階流派的基礎。

這個流派

目標：打完 Hell 15

這不是 Emperium Festival 流派，而是用弱裝備打完 Hell 15 的平價版本，方便你刷精煉、等級與 MVP 卡。裝備齊全後，Festival 請看進階攻略。
1治療觸發4 張 Mastering 在普攻時施放治療；8 張 Vitata 在受擊時施放。
2治療爆炸Quadrille 之魂讓每次治療變成神聖爆炸：幾乎是全部傷害來源。
3增益加乘Odin 4/4、Blessing、Steel Body、Signum Crucis EX 與 Meltdown 讓每次爆炸更強。

實證：通關 Hell 15

0.2.8 版 Glast Heim Hell 15（Frenzy ×2）：擊敗 Dark Lord、Fallen Randgris 與 Satan Morroc，20:20、等級 367 結束。
